Chain Reaction

Pass one phone around the table. Fill cells with orbs until they burst, steal your friends' cells, and be the last colour left on the board.

Players

4

Tap a name to rename it. Switch a seat to CPU to let the phone play it.

Board

How to play
  1. Players take turns. On your turn, tap an empty cell or a cell that already has your orbs.
  2. Every cell has a limit. A corner holds 1 orb, an edge holds 2, and a middle cell holds 3.
  3. Add one more and the cell explodes. It throws one orb into each neighbouring cell and turns those cells your colour.
  4. Explosions can set off more explosions, so one tap can flip half the board.
  5. Once everyone has played, a player with no orbs left is out. The last colour standing wins.
