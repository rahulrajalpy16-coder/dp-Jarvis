Questions

Common questions

What is an interactive 3D product demo?

It is a web page where buyers can rotate, zoom, look inside and take apart a 3D model of your product, with each part linked to its information. It runs in a normal browser, so there is nothing to download.

Can you use our existing CAD files?

Yes. CAD files are the best starting point. I convert and simplify them so the model looks right and loads quickly on a phone. Without CAD, I can model from drawings and photos.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. I optimise every model for mobile, and test it on real phones before handover.

Can it go on our existing website?

Yes. The demo can live on its own page, be embedded in your current site, or run full screen on a trade show display.

Is the PS-120 a real product?

No. It is a concept I modelled to demonstrate the technique, and its values are illustrative.
