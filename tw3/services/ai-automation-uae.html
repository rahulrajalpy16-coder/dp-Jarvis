Where it helps

The slow steps everyone hates

In most businesses, good people spend hours copying details from emails into spreadsheets, chasing approvals and rebuilding the same quote for the tenth time. That work is perfect for automation, because the steps are the same every time.

01

Enquiry to quote

An assistant reads a technical enquiry, pulls out the items, site and deadline, and drafts the quote for a person to approve.

02

Inbox triage

Incoming email is sorted, labelled and summarised, and urgent messages are flagged to the right person.

03

Documents and filing

Invoices, datasheets and certificates are read, renamed and filed, with the key numbers pulled into your sheet.

04

Approvals and follow ups

Requests go to the right approver, reminders go out on time, and nothing waits in someone’s inbox.

Questions

Common questions

What can AI automation do for a small business?

It takes over repetitive steps that follow the same pattern each time: reading enquiries, drafting replies and quotes, filing documents, updating spreadsheets and chasing approvals. Your team spends that time on customers instead.

Will the AI send things to customers without checking?

Not unless you want it to. I design workflows so a person approves anything important, such as a quote or a payment, before it goes out.

Do I need new software?

Usually not. I connect the tools you already use, such as your email, documents and spreadsheets.

How do we start?

We pick one slow, repetitive task, automate it, and measure the time saved. If it works, we move on to the next one.
