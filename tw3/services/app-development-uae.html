Questions

Common questions

What is a progressive web app?

It is a web app that installs onto a phone or computer from a link, has its own icon, works offline and updates itself, without going through an app store.

Can you build an app for iPhone and Android?

Yes. A progressive web app runs on both from one codebase. If you need a store listing or special phone features, we can discuss a native wrapper.

How long does a custom app take?

A focused tool usually takes three to six weeks. I start with the one screen your team needs most and build from there.

Who owns the code?

You do. At handover you get the code, the hosting details and plain language notes on how it works.
